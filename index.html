I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Explainable Machine Learning group at TUM and Helmholtz Munich, led by Prof. Zeynep Akata. I work on mechanistic interpretability of vision and language models, with a focus on AI safety and alignment. I completed my Ph.D. at TU Berlin (summa cum laude), following an M.Sc. at TU Berlin and TU Eindhoven and a B.Sc. at Saint Petersburg State University.

I study how mechanistic understanding can support scalable AI control. Through INVERT, PRISM, and CoSy, I develop methods to identify, evaluate, and causally test internal representations. My current work targets safety-relevant LLM states, with the longer-term goal of turning mechanistic signals into runtime monitoring and intervention tools for autonomous agents and interacting agent populations.
